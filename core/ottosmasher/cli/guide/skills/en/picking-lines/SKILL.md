# Pick dialogue for the intended use

Follow `otto guide workflow` for scope/precision and `otto guide audio` for real capability checks. Establish whether phrases may be recontextualized, who says each part, desired emotion, positive/negative valence, pitch relative to the character's baseline, and the requested tags. Reuse explicit instructions. A protagonist declaration spoken by the character cannot stand in for somebody addressing her.

## Search broadly, inspect selectively

Use textLayer.search for known phrases and local scripts on textLayer.list for semantic candidate collection. Keywords reduce volume but do not establish meaning. Expand synonyms, reactions and paraphrases, then inspect unexplored scenes/episodes when results cluster too narrowly. Keep only relevant candidates in context. A literal exact-match miss is not an exhaustive semantic search.

Check candidate audio, actual wording, speaker and emotional delivery. A high F0 or loud shout can mean fear, pain or frustration; compare ordinary speech by the same character and report the comparison method. For happy/proud/winning material actively inspect celebrations, successful actions, pleased laughter and self-introductions. When pictures matter, check the frames; spoken previews and offscreen dialogue may show someone else.

## Curate and finish

Reuse existing samples and analysis; create missing full lines or deliberately clipped phrases only after validating the choice. Retain source range, intended excerpt, speaker, meaning, selection reason and evidence. Apply requested tags through samples.batchEdit then edits.apply; an empty batch is already done and must not be applied. Report exact-match limitations and semantic alternatives clearly. Preserve prior selections unless the user asked to retract them. At fine precision review every agreed candidate, not just a few examples. App readiness is not evidence of semantic suitability.
